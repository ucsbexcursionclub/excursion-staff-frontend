**Authors:** Marisha Kapinska & Xinghan Yang

## Summary

Large-scale UI/UX refactor and feature additions across the entire staff frontend. Changes span search behavior, navigation layout, page aesthetics, data visualizations, and a new staff guide.

---

## 1. Real-Time Debounced Search

**Files:** `MembersNav.tsx`, `GearNav.tsx`, `StaffNav.tsx`

- Replaced the submit-on-Enter / Search-button pattern with a `useEffect` debounce (300 ms).
- Search results now filter as the user types — no need to spell the full name before results appear.
- Added a clear (✕) button that appears when there is input, allowing instant reset.
- This directly addresses the pain point of having to ask members to spell their full name during gear checkout or renewal.

## 3. Staff Guide Page (`/guide`)

**Files:** `GuidePage.tsx`, `App.tsx`, `NavBar.tsx`

- New staff-facing guide page, accessible to all staff/admin at `/guide`.
- Two tabs at the top:
  - **How to Use** — numbered accordion steps for every feature (Getting Started, Members, Gear, Trips, Stats, Admin).
  - **Resources** — merged Links page content; shows API-fetched link groups as category pills + card grid.
- Quick-nav chips at the top of the How-to-Use tab allow jumping to any section.
- Each section has an intro paragraph, expandable numbered steps, and a tips callout.

## 10. Stats Page — Data Visualizations
